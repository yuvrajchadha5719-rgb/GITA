Trade Integrity & Cost Control

Why Choose Us? Direct Verification, Buhler Sortex & In-House Logistics

We eliminate intermediary trading layers. International importers partner with our merchant export house, backed by a dedicated team of rice verification specialists, partner mills utilizing Buhler Sortex grading, and in-house customs and freight teams keeping landed container costs low.

01 / VERIFICATION

Specialist Rice Verification Team

Our dedicated on-ground team of rice specialists rigorously inspects every batch prior to bagging. We calibrate Average Grain Length (AGL), cooking elongation ratio, digital moisture (<12.5%), and purity levels to guarantee flawless container loads.

02 / SORTEX

Partner Mills with Buhler Sortex

Our manufacturing and processing partners across Haryana and Punjab utilize multi-stage Buhler optical Sortex grading. High-resolution CCD optical sensors scan every grain, eliminating discolored kernels, chalky grains, and impurities.

03 / FREIGHT

In-House Freight for Lower Landed Costs

Our dedicated in-house freight operations secure direct volume contracts with premier shipping lines (Maersk, MSC, Hapag-Lloyd). Bypassing external forwarders directly lowers landed container costs for our overseas buyers.

04 / CUSTOMS

In-House Customs Brokerage & Port Teams

With our own customs documentation and field clearance personnel stationed at Mundra, Nhava Sheva (JNPT), and Kandla, container gate-ins, vessel bookings, and customs inspections proceed rapidly without demurrage risks.

05 / AUDIT

Pre-Shipment Stuffing & Sealing Audits

Our QA team conducts mandatory stuffing audits at mill dispatch bays and port yards. We inspect food-grade container dry-liners, desiccants, bag stitching, tare weights, and fix tamper-evident customs export seals.

06 / STATUTORY

Statutory APEDA, GI & LC Compliance

Full regulatory assurance backed by APEDA Registration-cum-Allocation Certificates (RCAC), authentic GI certificates, Phytosanitary & Fumigation passes, SGS/Geo-Chem inspection, and global Letter of Credit (LC) trade terms.
