**Great Indian Trade Alliance (GITA)** is a premier, government-recognized Indian merchant export house specializing in containerized shipments of authentic Indian Basmati and Non-Basmati rice to global commercial buyers across the Middle East, Europe, North America, and Africa.

---

## 🌾 Core Export Rice Portfolio

- **1121 Basmati Rice** (Steam, White Sella, Golden Sella) — 8.35mm+ Average Grain Length (AGL), 2.5x cooking elongation.
- **1509 & 1401 Basmati Rice** (Steam, Sella) — 8.40mm+ slender grains, high-aroma culinary profile.
- **Traditional Aged Basmati** — 7.30mm+ vintage 1–2 year aged grains with APEDA GI certification.
- **IR64 & Non-Basmati Parboiled** — High-yield, cost-effective staples for mass distribution and institutional tenders.
- **PR11, PR14 & Sona Masoori** — Silky medium-slender grains with strict moisture control (<12.5%).
- **Turnkey Private-Label OEM Packaging** — 1kg to 50kg BOPP, non-woven, jute, and master corrugated cartons.
