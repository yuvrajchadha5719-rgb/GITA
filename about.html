About Great Indian Trade Alliance

Welcome to Great Indian Trade Alliance

Building dependable global food supply chains from India through advanced rice milling, verified production lines, and disciplined export execution.

Our Mission

Delivering Agricultural Excellence to Global Buyers

Our mission is to supply international food importers, distributors, and private label brands with authentic, premium-grade Indian Basmati and Non-Basmati rice direct from origin.

Through our integrated milling operations and farm-level procurement networks in Haryana and Punjab, we eliminate unnecessary middlemen. Great Indian Trade Alliance delivers consistent grain specifications, full APEDA statutory compliance, and transparent factory-direct commercial terms.

Who Are We

An Integrated Milling & Volume Export Enterprise

Great Indian Trade Alliance (GITA) is a premier Indian rice milling, processing, and bulk export enterprise supplying international distributors, wholesalers, and retail brands. We operate high-capacity automated milling lines, optical Sortex cleaners, and dedicated container dispatch to ensure pristine grain purity.

Our focus is direct manufacturer-to-buyer execution: transparent ex-mill or CIF pricing, counter-sample verification, and reliable containerized sailings.
