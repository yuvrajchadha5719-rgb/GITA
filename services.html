Processing Stages

From Raw Paddy to Sealed Export Container

Each shipment passes through our integrated six-stage processing and quality assurance framework before dispatch to global seaports.

01 / PRE-CLEAN

Pre-Cleaning & De-Stoning

Vibrating mechanical sieves, rotary aspiration separators, and magnetic de-stoners remove dust, stones, straw, and foreign seeds before paddy enters the hulling chambers.

02 / PARBOIL

Temperature-Controlled Parboiling

Automated soaking and pressurized steam chambers gelatinize the grain starch, driving nutrients into the endosperm and producing uniform golden or creamy sella rice.

03 / POLISH

Multi-Pass Silky Polishing

Gentle abrasive friction and water mist polishing gently remove the bran layer while preserving intact grain tips and the characteristic basmati curvature.

04 / SORTEX

Buhler Optical Color Sorting

Advanced CCD optical sensors scan every single grain at 15 metric tonnes per hour, rejecting discolored kernels, black tips, and foreign impurities with 100% precision.

05 / TESTING

Laboratory QA/QC & Cooking Trials

Digital caliper grain length (AGL) measurement, moisture calibration, chemical purity assays, and formal kitchen elongation trials accompany each commercial lot.

06 / PACKAGING

Automated OEM & Export Packaging

Automated bagging in 5kg, 10kg, 20kg, 25kg, and 50kg BOPP, non-woven, PP, and master cartons with custom buyer brand artwork, loaded directly into sea containers.
