Turnkey OEM Execution

Our 4-Step Private Label Process

From initial design artwork approval to container loading, we oversee every phase with meticulous precision.

01

Artwork & Proofing

You provide your vector logo and design files (or our graphics team assists). We create 3D mockups and prepare precision rotogravure cylinder plates.

02

Regulatory Review

We verify mandatory nutritional facts, ingredients, barcode registration, net weight declarations, and dual-language regulatory requirements for your target country.

03

Milling & Bag Printing

Bags are printed and cured while your specified rice variety (1121, 1509, Traditional, or IR64) undergoes Buhler Sortex cleaning and digital moisture calibration.

04

Stuffing & Port Dispatch

Automated bagging lines weigh, fill, and seal bags before palletization. Pre-shipment QA checks ensure immaculate container stuffing for ocean transit.
